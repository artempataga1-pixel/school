Как работать с референсами

1
Загрузи фото
Нажми скрепку в ChatGPT и загрузи картинки в том порядке, в каком будешь на них ссылаться.
2
Назови роли
В промпте картинки называются image 1, image 2, image 3. Первая загруженная это image 1.
3
Скопируй промпт
Выбери нужный ниже, нажми «Скопировать» и вставь в чат вместе с фото.
4
Правь кликом
Мелочи правь одной фразой, нажав на готовую картинку. Не начинай заново.
Одна фраза, которая спасает: «Keep the person's face and appearance from image 1.» Добавляй её, когда на картинке человек.
С чьими лицами можно работать. Только свои фото или фото людей, которые согласились. Никаких фейковых снимков со знаменитостями и никаких «событий», которых не было, в рекламе и новостях. Для клиентских работ подписывай, что изображение сгенерировано ИИ.

Раскадровки

Раскадровка по твоей идее

Приложи (по желанию): героя, одежду, предмет, локацию или стиль. Подставь свой сценарий и настройки в скобках.

Create one clean cinematic storyboard sheet with short captions.

SETTINGS
Planned video length: [8-15 seconds]
Number of keyframes: [9]
Frame aspect ratio: [16:9 / 9:16 / 1:1]

STORY
[Paste your script, idea or short action here]

REFERENCES
Work out the role of each attached image yourself: character, outfit, object, product, location or style. Keep each referenced character's face, build, outfit and accessories recognizable. Keep products and objects true to shape, color and material. Use a location reference for space, light and atmosphere. Use a style reference only for palette, grading and mood, never for its content. If there are no references, design the characters yourself and keep them identical in every frame.

STORY STRUCTURE
Make exactly the number of frames set above. Each frame shows one meaningful action, change, reaction or detail; together they read as one story, not as similar poses. For nine frames follow this arc: situation, hero or object, impulse, action begins, development, detail or reaction, change, climax, final image. Vary shot size and angle on purpose: wide, medium, close-up, macro, POV, over-the-shoulder, low and high angle. Do not add characters, objects, logos, speech bubbles or text that are not in the story.

LAYOUT
One sheet in an even grid (4 frames: 2x2, 6: 3x2, 9: 3x3, 12: 4x3). Every cell has the frame on top and a narrow caption bar directly below it with one short Russian caption of 4-10 words, starting with the frame number ("1.", "2." ...). Captions are large, sharp and fully legible. No other text on the sheet: no title, no text inside the frames, no logos, no watermarks. All cells the same size, aligned, even gutters, nothing cropped.
Раскадровки

Раскадровка: история по картинке

Приложи одну картинку: персонаж, место, предмет или стиль. ChatGPT придумает историю сам.

From the attached image, invent a strong, clear, original story for a short AI video of about 15 seconds, then draw a cinematic storyboard for it. Do not ask clarifying questions.

First decide what the image is mainly about: a character, a location, an object, a product or a visual style, and build the story on that. The story must work without dialogue or on-screen text and have an emotional hook, one main action, one small unexpected turn and a strong final image. It must follow naturally from what is visible in the image.
If the image shows a character, keep the face, age, build, hairstyle, clothing and mood. If it shows a product or object, keep its shape, color and material. If it shows a location, keep the space, time of day, light and palette. Do not add new main characters unless the story needs them.

Write first, as plain text: 1) a one-sentence logline, 2) a 2-3 sentence story, 3) one sentence on why this story fits this image.

Then create one clean storyboard sheet: 9 frames in a 3x3 grid, each frame 16:9 with a short Russian caption bar directly below it (4-10 words, a concrete action, starting with the frame number: "1.", "2." ...). Build the sequence like a director, not by formula: opening situation, detail or impulse, the hero's decision, action begins, development, reaction or key detail, unexpected change, climax, final image. Every frame must be needed and differ in action, composition, shot size or state. Use wide, medium, close-up, macro, POV, low or high angles only when they strengthen the meaning. No other text on the sheet: no title, no text inside frames, no speech bubbles, logos or watermarks. Keep one consistent look taken from the reference.

After the image, add a numbered list of the 9 frames, one line each: action, shot or angle, and a short camera move.

You are a creative director and carousel designer who prepares prompts for GPT Image. Design a SEAMLESS carousel: a series of wide spreads where one image covers two neighboring slides. Each spread will later be cut in half, so each half must work as a complete slide on its own while the two halves read as one continuous scene. Do not generate images now.

<project_data>
Topic, goal, target audience, platform, number of slides, aspect ratio of one slide, spread format, facts, brand name, signature, logo, references, visual style, must include, must not include.
</project_data>
If something is missing, fill it in sensibly from the topic.

RULES
- One slide is one final slide. One spread is one wide image for two slides. A 4:5 slide (1080x1350) means a spread of 8:5 (2160x1350).
- The vertical center line is the future cut. Backgrounds, light, large shapes, silhouettes, textures, smoke, brush strokes and large objects may cross it.
- Never put across the cut line: key words, small text, the logo, the slide number, the progress indicator, a close-up face, or any critical part of the composition. Keep a "cut zone" around the center free of small text.
- Each half has its own safe zone and its own complete composition. Logo, signature, number and progress indicator belong to each individual slide and sit in the same place on every slide.
- If the number of slides is odd, propose one solution: a standalone last slide, an asymmetric final spread, or a final spread with one real slide and one decorative ending.

ANSWER IN THIS ORDER
1. CREATIVE CONCEPT: main idea, visual metaphor, emotional effect, narrative logic, how the transition between slides works, what holds attention while swiping.
2. FIXED DESIGN SYSTEM: everything a normal carousel needs (palette, textures, typography, positions of logo, signature, number and progress indicator, recurring elements, grid, margins, forbidden changes) plus safe zones and cut-zone rules.
3. SPREAD ARCHITECTURE: for each spread (SPREAD_01 ...): which slides it holds (CARD_01 + CARD_02), its role, the shared seamless visual, what is in the left half, what is in the right half, what crosses the cut, and what must never sit on the cut.
4. SLIDE SCRIPT: for each CARD_xx: its spread, role, exact headline, exact text, signature, number, progress-indicator state, scene, layout, link to the neighboring slide, and how it stands alone after the cut.
5. REFERENCE SPREAD: choose one spread (not a single slide) that best shows the design system and the seamless transition, and explain why.
6. READY PROMPTS: one for the reference spread and one for every other spread. Each states the spread aspect ratio, that it is one wide image for two slides, the IDs, the exact left and right text with exact positions, the shared visual, the left and right halves, how the visual crosses the center, a ban on important elements on the cut line, logo, signature, number and progress state per slide, the repeating UI elements, the fixed design rules, and that the image will be cut in half and each half must be a complete slide.
7. BATCH PROMPT for all spreads except the reference: a separate wide image and a separate file per spread, no collage, everything in one task, the approved reference spread as a strict visual reference, exact text only, nothing skipped, IDs on the results, a seamless visual between the halves, no important text on the cut line.

FORMAT: first a short summary of the idea, then all sections in order. Put every GPT Image prompt and the batch prompt in separate code blocks.
